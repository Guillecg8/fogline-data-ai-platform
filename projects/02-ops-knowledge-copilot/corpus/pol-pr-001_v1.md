# Dynamic Pricing Guardrails

## Purpose
Set the limits within which room rates may be changed, whether by the revenue manager or by the pricing model.

## Rate limits
The nightly rate may not go below 70% or above 250% of the base rate for the room type and season.

## Daily change limit
A change of more than 20% in one day for the same stay date requires approval from the revenue manager.

## Model recommendations
Recommendations from the forecasting model are applied automatically only when they are inside both limits. Anything outside goes to the revenue manager's review queue.

## Rate parity
Rates on the Fogline website may never be higher than the rates offered on third-party channels.
