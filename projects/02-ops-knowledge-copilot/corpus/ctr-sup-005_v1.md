# Supply Agreement Summary: Sonoma Fields Produce Cooperative

## Parties and term
Agreement between Fogline Hospitality Group and Sonoma Fields Produce Cooperative for fresh fruit and vegetables for Saltgrass and Lumen. The term runs from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027. It replaces the expired agreement with Green Valley Produce.

## Deliveries
Deliveries take place every day except Sunday, between 5:00 and 7:00 am. The minimum order is $200 per delivery.

## Organic share
At least 60% of the produce supplied each quarter, by value, must be certified organic.

## Payment
Invoices are payable net 30 days.

## Shortages
The supplier notifies the kitchen of any shortage by 3:00 pm the day before delivery and proposes a substitute.

## Termination
Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.
