# Check-in and Check-out

## Purpose
Define standard arrival and departure times and the fees for exceptions.

## Standard times
Check-in starts at 3:00 pm. Check-out is at 11:00 am.

## Early check-in
Early check-in is available from 12:00 pm, subject to availability, for a fee of $35.

## Late check-out
Late check-out until 2:00 pm is available, subject to availability, for a fee of $45. After 2:00 pm a half-day rate applies.

## Loyalty exceptions
Fogline Circle Gold members receive early check-in and late check-out free of charge when available.

## Identification
A government-issued photo ID and a payment card are required at check-in. A deposit hold of $100 per stay is placed for incidentals.
