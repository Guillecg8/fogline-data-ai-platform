# Health Inspection Readiness

## Purpose
Keep both restaurants ready for an unannounced inspection by the San Francisco Department of Public Health.

## Weekly self-audit
The restaurant general manager completes the self-audit checklist every Monday before service. The checklist follows the categories used in public inspection reports.

## Corrective actions
High-risk findings must be corrected within 48 hours. Moderate and low-risk findings must be corrected within 7 days. Each finding gets an owner and a due date in the audit tracker.

## During an inspection
The manager on duty accompanies the inspector, takes notes, and does not argue findings on site. A copy of the report is sent to the director of operations the same day.

## Posting
The most recent inspection report is kept available for any guest who asks to see it.
