# Supply Agreement Summary: Ridgeline Coffee Roasters

## Parties and term
Agreement between Fogline Hospitality Group and Ridgeline Coffee Roasters for roasted coffee for Lumen and Fogline Pantry. The term runs from April 1, 2025 to March 31, 2027.

## Product
The supplier roasts the exclusive Fogline House blend, which it may not sell to other customers in San Francisco.

## Volume and price
The wholesale price is $9.80 per pound. The minimum volume is 120 pounds per month.

## Deliveries
Coffee is delivered every Monday, roasted no more than 5 days before delivery.

## Payment
Invoices are payable net 15 days.

## Termination
Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice.
