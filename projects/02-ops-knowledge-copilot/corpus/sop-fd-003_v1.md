# Guest Complaints and Compensation

## Purpose
Resolve guest complaints quickly and apply compensation consistently.

## Approval limits
A front desk agent may offer compensation worth up to $75 per stay. The duty manager may approve up to $300. Anything above $300 requires the hotel general manager.

## Steps
Listen, apologize, fix the problem, and then offer compensation that fits the issue. Do not offer compensation before trying to fix the problem.

## Logging
Every complaint is logged in the incident tracker within 2 hours, including the compensation given and who approved it.

## Follow-up
The duty manager contacts the guest within 24 hours for any complaint involving safety, cleanliness or billing.
