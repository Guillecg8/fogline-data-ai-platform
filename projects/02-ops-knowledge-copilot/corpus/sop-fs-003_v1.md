# Allergen Handling

## Purpose
Prevent allergic reactions among guests at Saltgrass and Lumen.

## Allergens covered
The procedure covers the nine major allergens: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame.

## Taking the order
Servers ask every table about allergies. An allergy order is flagged in the point-of-sale system and the manager on duty confirms it with the kitchen before it is fired.

## Preparation
Allergy orders are prepared with purple-handled utensils and a purple cutting board, on a cleaned and sanitized surface. The chef who prepares the dish plates it and hands it directly to the server.

## Menu changes
The allergen matrix must be updated within 24 hours of any menu or recipe change. The executive chef approves the update.
