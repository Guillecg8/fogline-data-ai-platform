# Guest Room Cleaning Standard

## Purpose
Set the cleaning standard and time targets for guest rooms at Fogline hotels.

## Time targets
A stayover room is allotted 20 minutes. A checkout room is allotted 30 minutes.

## Linen
Bed linen in stayover rooms is changed daily. Towels are replaced daily.

## Inspection
Supervisors inspect 100% of checkout rooms and a random 10% of stayover rooms.
