# Restaurant Reservations and No-shows

## Scope
Applies to reservations at Saltgrass. Lumen does not take reservations.

## Deposits
Parties of 6 or more guests pay a deposit of $25 per person when booking. The deposit is deducted from the final bill.

## Cancellations
Reservations may be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the booking time. After that the deposit is kept.

## Late arrivals
Tables are held for 15 minutes after the booking time. After 15 minutes the table may be released and the booking is treated as a no-show.

## Large parties
Parties of more than 12 guests are handled by the events coordinator under a separate event agreement.
