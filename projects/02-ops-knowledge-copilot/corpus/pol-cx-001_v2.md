# Hotel Cancellation Policy

## Scope
Applies to all direct bookings at Fogline Marina and Fogline Mission made on or after April 1, 2026.

## Flexible rate
Guests may cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before the check-in time on the arrival date. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged the first night plus tax.

## Non-refundable rate
The advance purchase rate is charged in full at booking and is not refundable or changeable.

## Group bookings
Reservations of 8 or more rooms are group bookings. They may be cancelled free of charge up to 14 days before arrival.

## Exceptions
The duty manager may waive a cancellation fee for documented emergencies. Waivers are logged in the incident tracker.
