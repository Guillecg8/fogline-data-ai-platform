# Cold Storage Temperature Control

## Purpose
Keep temperature-controlled foods out of the danger zone in every Fogline kitchen.

## Temperature limits
Walk-in and reach-in refrigerators must hold food at 41°F (5°C) or below. Freezers must hold 0°F (-18°C) or below.

## Logging
Each unit is checked and logged every 4 hours during operating hours, starting at opening. Readings go into the digital temperature log on the kitchen tablet. The sous chef on duty signs the log at the end of each shift.

## Corrective action
If a refrigerator reads above 41°F, move the food to a working unit and recheck after 30 minutes. Any temperature-controlled food that has been above 41°F for more than 2 hours must be discarded and recorded in the waste log. Report the faulty unit to maintenance as an urgent request.

## Responsibility
The executive chef owns this procedure. The restaurant general manager reviews the logs every Monday.
