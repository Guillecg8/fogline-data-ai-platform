# Overbooking and Guest Relocation

## Purpose
Limit overbooking and protect guests when the hotel cannot honor a reservation.

## Overbooking limits
Overbooking may not exceed 4% of available rooms on weekdays (Sunday to Thursday) and 2% on weekends (Friday and Saturday). The revenue manager sets the daily level within these limits.

## Relocating a guest
If a guest must be relocated, the duty manager books a room at a partner hotel of equal or higher category. Fogline pays for the first night and for transport to the partner hotel. The guest also receives a $50 credit for a future stay.

## Who is relocated
Never relocate Fogline Circle Gold members, guests staying more than three nights, or guests who have already been relocated once.

## Recording
Every relocation is logged in the incident tracker before the end of the shift.
