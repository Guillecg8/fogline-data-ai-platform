# Hotel Cancellation Policy

## Scope
Applies to all direct bookings at Fogline hotels.

## Flexible rate
Guests may cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the check-in time on the arrival date. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged the first night plus tax.

## Non-refundable rate
The advance purchase rate is charged in full at booking and is not refundable.

## Group bookings
Reservations of 10 or more rooms may be cancelled free of charge up to 7 days before arrival.
