# Incident and Injury Reporting

## Purpose
Make sure injuries and safety incidents are reported and followed up.

## Immediate report
Any injury to a guest or employee is reported to the duty manager within 1 hour. Call emergency services first when the situation requires it.

## Written report
The employee involved, or the witness, completes the incident form within 24 hours. The duty manager adds a short description of the actions taken.

## Employee injuries
The duty manager informs People Operations on the same day so the workers' compensation claim can be opened.

## Review
The director of operations reviews all incidents monthly and shares the patterns with venue managers.
