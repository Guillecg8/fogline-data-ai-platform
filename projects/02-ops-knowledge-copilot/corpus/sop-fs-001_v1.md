# Cold Storage Temperature Control

## Purpose
Keep temperature-controlled foods out of the danger zone in every Fogline kitchen.

## Temperature limits
Refrigerators must hold food at 41°F (5°C) or below. Freezers must hold 0°F (-18°C) or below.

## Logging
Each unit is checked and logged twice per shift on the paper temperature sheet kept on the walk-in door.

## Corrective action
If a refrigerator reads above 41°F, move the food to a working unit. Food that has been above 41°F for more than 4 hours must be discarded.

## Responsibility
The executive chef owns this procedure.
