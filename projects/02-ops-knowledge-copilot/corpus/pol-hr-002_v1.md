# Tip Pooling in Restaurants

## Scope
Applies to hourly service staff at Saltgrass and Lumen. Managers and supervisors do not take part in the tip pool.

## Distribution
Pooled tips are divided as follows: 65% to servers and baristas, 20% to kitchen staff, and 15% to bussers and hosts. Each group's share is split by hours worked in the pay period.

## Payment
Tips are paid with regular payroll every two weeks. Card tips are paid in full with no processing fee deducted.

## Records
The restaurant general manager keeps the tip pool calculation for each pay period for 3 years.
