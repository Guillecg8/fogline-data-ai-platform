# Supply Agreement Summary: Pacific Tide Seafood Co.

## Parties and term
Agreement between Fogline Hospitality Group and Pacific Tide Seafood Co. for fresh and frozen seafood for Saltgrass. The term runs from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.

## Deliveries
Deliveries take place on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday between 6:00 and 8:00 am. The minimum order is $400 per delivery.

## Pricing
Prices follow the supplier's weekly price list and are reviewed quarterly.

## Payment
Invoices are payable net 30 days.

## Quality and credits
Fogline may reject product that fails the receiving checks. Credit for rejected product must be requested within 24 hours of delivery.

## Termination
Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.
