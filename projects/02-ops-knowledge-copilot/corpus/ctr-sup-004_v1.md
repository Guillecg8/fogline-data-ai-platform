# Supply Agreement Summary: Green Valley Produce

## Parties and term
Agreement between Fogline Hospitality Group and Green Valley Produce for fresh fruit and vegetables. The term ran from January 1, 2024 to December 31, 2025 and was not renewed.

## Deliveries
Deliveries took place Monday to Friday between 5:00 and 7:00 am. The minimum order was $150 per delivery.

## Payment
Invoices were payable net 21 days.

## Termination
Either party could terminate with 30 days' written notice.
