# Guest Room Cleaning Standard

## Purpose
Set the cleaning standard and time targets for guest rooms at Fogline Marina and Fogline Mission.

## Time targets
A stayover room is allotted 20 minutes. A checkout room is allotted 35 minutes. Suites receive 15 additional minutes.

## Linen
Under the Fogline Green Stay program, bed linen in stayover rooms is changed every third night, or sooner if the guest asks. Towels left on the floor are replaced daily.

## Inspection
Supervisors inspect 100% of checkout rooms and a random 20% of stayover rooms. A room is not released to the front desk until it passes inspection.

## Do not disturb
If a room shows a do-not-disturb sign for more than 48 hours, housekeeping informs the duty manager, who performs a welfare check.
