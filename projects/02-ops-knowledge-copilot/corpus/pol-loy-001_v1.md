# Fogline Circle Loyalty Program

## Tiers
Members reach Silver after 5 nights and Gold after 15 nights in a calendar year. Tier status lasts through the end of the following calendar year.

## Points
Members earn 10 points per dollar spent on rooms and 5 points per dollar spent at Saltgrass, Lumen and Fogline Pantry. Points expire after 24 months without activity.

## Silver benefits
Silver members receive a welcome drink and a 10% discount at Lumen.

## Gold benefits
Gold members receive free early check-in and late check-out when available, a room upgrade when available, and a 15% discount at Saltgrass and Lumen.

## Redemption
A free night starts at 20,000 points.
