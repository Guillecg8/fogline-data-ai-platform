# Service Agreement Summary: Bayfront Linen Services

## Parties and term
Agreement between Fogline Hospitality Group and Bayfront Linen Services for linen rental and laundry for both hotels. The term runs from July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2027.

## Service
Soiled linen is collected and clean linen delivered daily, including weekends.

## Service level
The supplier guarantees a 98% fill rate per delivery. If the monthly fill rate falls below 98%, Fogline receives a 5% credit on that month's invoice.

## Payment
Invoices are issued monthly and payable net 45 days.

## Lost and damaged linen
Linen lost or damaged beyond normal wear is charged at the replacement price list attached to the agreement.

## Termination
Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice.
