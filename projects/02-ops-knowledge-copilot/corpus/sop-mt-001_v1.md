# Preventive Maintenance and Repair Requests

## Purpose
Keep equipment reliable and respond to repair requests within agreed times.

## Response times
Urgent requests, such as a failed refrigerator, a water leak or a guest locked out, are answered within 30 minutes. Routine requests are completed within 24 hours.

## Preventive schedule
HVAC filters are replaced quarterly. Elevators are serviced monthly by the certified vendor. Kitchen hood systems are cleaned every 6 months. Fire extinguishers are checked monthly.

## Rooms out of order
A room that cannot be sold is marked out of order in the property system by engineering, with an expected return date. The front desk may not sell it until engineering releases it.
