# Receiving Seafood Deliveries

## Purpose
Make sure only safe, fresh seafood enters the Saltgrass kitchen.

## Delivery window
Seafood is received between 6:00 and 8:00 am only. A trained receiver must be present; drivers may not leave product unattended.

## Checks on arrival
Measure the internal temperature of fresh fish with a probe thermometer. Reject any fresh fish above 41°F. Frozen product must arrive frozen solid with no signs of thawing. Live shellfish must arrive with the harvester tag attached.

## Shellfish tags
Keep every shellfish tag for 90 days after the container is emptied. Tags are filed by date in the receiving binder.

## Rejections
Record rejected product on the delivery note, take a photo, and notify the supplier the same day so a credit can be requested.
