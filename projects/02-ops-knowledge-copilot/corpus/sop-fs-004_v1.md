# Cleaning and Sanitizing Food Contact Surfaces

## Purpose
Define how food contact surfaces are cleaned and sanitized.

## Frequency
Food contact surfaces in continuous use are cleaned and sanitized at least every 4 hours, and always when switching between raw and ready-to-eat foods.

## Sanitizer concentration
Fogline kitchens use a quaternary ammonium sanitizer at 200 to 400 ppm. Concentration is verified with test strips at the start of each shift and whenever a new batch is mixed.

## Method
Wash with detergent, rinse with clean water, apply sanitizer, and let the surface air dry. Do not wipe sanitizer off with a towel.

## Records
Test strip results are written on the sanitizer log next to the three-compartment sink.
