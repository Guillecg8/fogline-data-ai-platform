# Scheduling and Time Off

## Schedules
Work schedules are posted at least 14 days in advance. Changes made after posting require the employee's agreement.

## Shift swaps
Employees may swap shifts with a qualified colleague. The manager must approve the swap at least 24 hours before the shift starts.

## Paid time off
Hourly employees accrue 1 hour of paid time off for every 30 hours worked. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days off must be submitted 21 days in advance.

## Blackout dates
Time off is not normally approved between December 20 and January 2, or during citywide convention weeks announced by the revenue team.
