# Purchasing and Expense Approvals

## Approval limits
Purchases up to $500 are approved by the department manager. Purchases from $500 to $5,000 require the venue general manager. Purchases above $5,000 require the chief financial officer.

## Quotes
Any purchase above $2,500 from a supplier without a contract requires three written quotes.

## Expense reports
Employee expenses are submitted within 30 days with an itemized receipt. Expenses without a receipt are not reimbursed above $25.

## Contracted suppliers
Orders placed under an active supplier contract follow the terms of that contract and do not need separate quotes.
