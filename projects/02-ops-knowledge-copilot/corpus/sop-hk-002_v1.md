# Lost and Found

## Purpose
Handle items left behind by hotel guests in a consistent and traceable way.

## Logging
Any found item is handed to the housekeeping office and logged within 24 hours with the date, room number and finder's name.

## Storage periods
Ordinary items are kept for 90 days. Valuables worth more than $100, including electronics, jewelry and cash, are stored in the front office safe and kept for 180 days. Perishable items are discarded after 24 hours.

## Returning items
The front desk contacts the guest once using the details on the reservation. Shipping is arranged at the guest's expense. Identification is required for collection in person.

## Unclaimed items
After the storage period, unclaimed items are donated to a local charity chosen by the hotel general manager.
