# Fogline Pantry Returns and Refunds

## Scope
Applies to orders placed on the Fogline Pantry online shop.

## Non-perishable items
Unopened non-perishable items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The customer pays return shipping unless the item arrived damaged.

## Perishable items
Perishable items cannot be returned. If a perishable item arrives spoiled or damaged, the customer must report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo, and receives a refund or a replacement.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the return is approved.

## Shipping area
Fogline Pantry ships only within the contiguous United States.
