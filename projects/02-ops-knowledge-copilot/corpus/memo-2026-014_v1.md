# Temporary Check-in Time at Fogline Marina During Renovation

## What changes
During the renovation of the third and fourth floors at Fogline Marina, check-in starts at 4:00 pm instead of 3:00 pm. Check-out stays at 11:00 am.

## Dates
The change applies to arrivals from September 1, 2026 to November 30, 2026. From December 1, 2026 the standard time in the check-in procedure applies again.

## Scope
The change applies only to Fogline Marina. Fogline Mission keeps the standard 3:00 pm check-in.

## Early check-in
Paid early check-in is suspended at Fogline Marina during this period. Gold members may store luggage and use the lounge from 12:00 pm.
