# Change of Seafood Delivery Days at Saltgrass

## What changes
From October 5, 2026, Pacific Tide Seafood Co. delivers to Saltgrass on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. This replaces the Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday schedule in the supply agreement.

## Why
Saltgrass now closes on Sundays, so the Saturday delivery left too much product over the closed day.

## What stays the same
The delivery window remains 6:00 to 8:00 am. The minimum order, payment terms and credit rules in the agreement are unchanged.

## Contract status
The change was agreed in writing as Amendment 1 to the supply agreement, signed on September 28, 2026.
