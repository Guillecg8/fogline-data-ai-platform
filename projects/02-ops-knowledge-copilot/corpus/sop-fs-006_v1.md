# Cooling Cooked Food

## Purpose
Cool cooked food fast enough to prevent bacterial growth.

## Two-stage cooling
Cooked food must cool from 135°F to 70°F within 2 hours, and from 70°F to 41°F within the next 4 hours. Total cooling time may not exceed 6 hours.

## Methods
Use shallow pans no deeper than 2 inches, ice baths, or the blast chiller. Stir liquids with an ice wand. Never cool large batches in a deep stock pot.

## Monitoring
Record the temperature at the start, at 2 hours and at 6 hours on the cooling log. If food misses the 2-hour target, reheat it once to 165°F and restart cooling, or discard it.
