# Guest Data Privacy and Retention

## Principles
Fogline collects only the guest data it needs to provide the service and keeps it only as long as necessary.

## Identification documents
Guest IDs are checked visually at check-in. Scans or photos of IDs are not stored.

## Payment data
Card numbers are tokenized by the payment processor. Employees never write down or email full card numbers.

## Retention
Security camera footage is kept for 30 days. Guest profiles with no stay or purchase for 3 years are deleted. Reservation and invoice records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes.

## Guest requests
Requests to access or delete personal data are answered within 30 days by the privacy lead.

## Internal tools
Analytics and AI tools may use only aggregated or de-identified guest data unless the privacy lead approves otherwise.
